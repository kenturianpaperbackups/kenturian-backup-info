Kenturian Backup App

This is a private tool used by Kenturian Pulp & Paper Mills Limited to back up its own company files to the company's Google Drive. It runs on company computers using the open-source program rclone.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only has access to the one Google Drive account that the company signs it in to.
