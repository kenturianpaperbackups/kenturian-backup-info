Privacy Policy

Kenturian Backup App. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

Who uses this app

This app is operated by Kenturian Pulp & Paper Mills Limited ("the company") for its own internal use. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the company signs in to it.

What the app does

The app uses the open-source program rclone to copy company files from company computers to a Google Drive account owned by the company. It asks for access to Google Drive so it can create and update backup files there.

What information it handles

The app handles only the company's own files and the sign-in token that lets it reach the company's backup Google Drive account. It does not collect information about any other person.

How information is used and shared

Files and tokens are used only to run the company's backups. They are not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model. Files are stored in the company's Google Drive, and sign-in tokens are stored on the company's own computers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (third-party access), and backed-up files can be deleted from Google Drive.
